## Data Fairness Reflection

### Basis for Judging Under-Representation

Under-representation is judged by the checkout count per member in each neighborhood. This rate is more informative than checkout totals alone because the neighborhoods do not have the same number of members.

### Finding

Zamalek is the least represented neighborhood by this measure, with 64 checkouts across 14 members, or 4.57 checkouts per member. The comparison includes all five neighborhoods in the cleaned data: Heliopolis, Maadi, Nasr City, Shubra, and Zamalek.

### Plausible Reason

A plausible reason is that Zamalek members may have less access to library events, transportation, or information about the checkout service. The checkout data alone cannot establish which explanation is true.

### Reasonable Next Step

Investigate checkout access in Zamalek through a short member survey and service review. Then run targeted outreach or a mobile-library trial there and compare the checkout-per-member rate again.
